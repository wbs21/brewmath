Privacy policy

Your brewing data belongs to you.

BrewMath is built without a developer account system, advertising network or analytics SDK. This policy explains what is stored, where it goes and when Apple or iCloud is involved.

Effective 24 November 2026 · Applies to BrewMath for iPhone and iPad

BrewMath does not collect personal data for the developer.

No account. No developer-operated server. No analytics. No advertising. No third-party tracking SDK.

Information stored on your device

BrewMath stores the information you enter in the app's local container, including:

  • batches, recipe snapshots, process values and preferences;
  • gravity readings, status changes and notes;
  • tasting ratings, verdicts and experiment attributions;
  • backup files you create and local recovery snapshots.

This information is not sent to a BrewMath server. The developer has no account, dashboard or remote method to read it.

iCloud Drive backups

BrewMath can copy backup files to the app's iCloud Drive container when iCloud Drive is enabled and your device is signed in to iCloud. Those files are stored in your own Apple iCloud account under your agreement with Apple.

The developer does not receive access to that container and cannot inspect, recover or delete your iCloud files remotely. You can manage these files through the Files app or through Apple's iCloud controls.

Backups and sharing

BrewMath can create human-readable JSON backup files. A backup remains under your control: you may keep it on the device, copy it to iCloud Drive, share it through the system share sheet or store it elsewhere.

A backup may contain batch names, recipes, notes and tasting information. Treat it as private data and review it before sending it to anyone, including support.

Purchases

BrewMath Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. BrewMath checks whether a valid entitlement exists so it can unlock or restore paid features.

The developer does not receive your name, payment method, Apple Account credentials or full billing details. Apple handles that transaction under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics and analytics

BrewMath contains no developer analytics, crash-reporting SDK, advertising SDK, social login or behavioural tracking. The developer does not create a profile of your brewing activity.

Apple may provide aggregated App Store information such as downloads, sales reports and crash information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. BrewMath itself does not collect that information for the developer.

External links

BrewMath and this website may link to public sources such as BJCP guideline documents, brewery or ingredient-manufacturer pages, or Apple's policies. Opening an external link leaves BrewMath and is governed by that website's own privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

Local BrewMath data remains on your device until you delete it inside the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to normal iOS and device-backup behaviour.

Backup files may remain in iCloud Drive or another location you selected until you delete them separately. Because the developer has no copy of your brewing data, there is no remote account to delete.

Children

BrewMath is a brewing calculator intended for adults of legal drinking age. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your data is protected by the security of your device, Apple account and the storage location you choose. Keep your device passcode and Apple Account secure, and store sensitive backups appropriately.

Changes to this policy

If BrewMath's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For a privacy question, email 13911100061@163.com. Do not include private recipe notes unless they are necessary to explain the issue.

Plain-language summary

BrewMath has no account and no developer analytics. Your batches, notes and backups stay under your control. StoreKit purchases are handled by Apple, and optional iCloud backups live in your own iCloud Drive.
