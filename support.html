Before contacting support

These checks solve most problems without affecting your batch data:

  1. Update to the latest version of BrewMath and iOS.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Confirm that the device has free storage space.
  4. If a purchase is missing, use Restore Purchase inside BrewMath.
  5. Before restoring a backup, keep the existing backup files unchanged.

If BrewMath cannot open its library

Do not delete the app or manually remove its files. The recovery screen is designed to preserve the existing database and backups.

Try the Try again button first. If the message returns, send us:

  • the exact error text shown by the app;
  • the iPhone or iPad model and iOS version;
  • the BrewMath version shown in Settings or About;
  • whether the app was recently restored from another device or backup.

Restore a BrewMath Pro purchase

Open the BrewMath Pro screen and tap Restore Purchase. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account used to buy them. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account and that StoreKit can reach the App Store.

BrewMath cannot see your name, payment method or Apple Account details.

iCloud Drive backups

BrewMath always writes a local backup. When iCloud Drive is available and the device is signed in to iCloud, a second copy can be written to BrewMath's iCloud Drive container.

  • Check that iCloud Drive is enabled in iOS Settings.
  • Check that the device has an active network connection.
  • Look in the Files app under iCloud Drive → BrewMath → Documents → Backups.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data; separately stored iCloud files may need to be removed in Files.

Calculation differences

IBU, ABV, colour, gravity and water calculations are estimates. Differences can come from hop age and form, wort volume, gravity, pH, measurement method, ingredient specifications and equipment losses.

When two established models disagree, BrewMath shows alternative results rather than presenting one result as laboratory truth. See Data sources and formulas for the references currently used.

Importing and restoring data

BrewMath validates a backup before changing the library. If validation fails, the file is rejected rather than partially restored.

When reporting an import problem, include the error message and, if possible, the backup's schema version. Do not send a backup that contains private notes unless you are comfortable sharing those notes with support.

Feature requests and corrections

For a data correction, include the ingredient or style, the current value, the proposed value and a public source URL. For a feature request, describe the brewing task and what currently gets in the way.
